📐 Cosine Similarity Formula

The vector distance between two games A and B is computed as:

$$\text{Similarity}(A, B) = \cos(\theta) = \frac{\mathbf{A} \cdot \mathbf{B}}{\|\mathbf{A}\| \|\mathbf{B}\|}$$
